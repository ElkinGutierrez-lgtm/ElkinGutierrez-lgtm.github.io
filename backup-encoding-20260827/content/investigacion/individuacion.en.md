---
title: "Individuation"
description: "Ontogenesis, transduction, the preindividual and processes of constitution."
---

Research on individuation as a process through which individuals emerge from metastable fields of potentiality.

The project focuses on ontogenesis, transduction, the preindividual and the relational conditions through which individual entities become constituted.

The central question is not simply what an individual is, but how individuation takes place.

---
title: "Pedagogy"
---

Pedagogical research addresses processes of formation, individuation, and the constitution of subjects and forms of knowledge.

Education is approached from an ontogenetic perspective: formation does not simply mean the transmission of content, but intervention in processes that transform capacities, relations, and modes of existence.

Particular attention is given to the relation between pedagogy, technology, and individuation, as well as to the institutional conditions in which educational processes take place.

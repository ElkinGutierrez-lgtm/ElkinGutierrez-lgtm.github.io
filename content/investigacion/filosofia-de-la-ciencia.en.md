---
title: "Philosophy of Science"
---

The philosophy of science examines the conditions under which scientific knowledge is produced, validated, and transformed. This research addresses epistemological, ontological, and historical problems concerning the constitution of scientific forms of knowledge.

Particular attention is given to the genesis of scientific concepts, the individuation of objects of knowledge, and the relations between science, technology, and institutions.

Science is thus approached not as a closed system of propositions, but as a dynamic field of problems, practices, and operations of knowledge production.

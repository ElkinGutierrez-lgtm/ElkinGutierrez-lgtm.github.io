# Psychology, Subjectivity and Individuation

This research line examines the constitution and transformation of subjectivity from a philosophical perspective. It addresses the affective, psychic and relational processes through which a subject is constituted, transformed and situated in relation to itself, others and its environment.

The research brings together problems of **individuation, identity, desire, anxiety, memory, affectivity and experience** with contributions from phenomenology, psychoanalysis, philosophical psychology and contemporary philosophy.

The aim is not to reduce subjectivity to a preconstituted psychological entity, but to investigate the conditions and processes through which forms of subjectivity emerge, stabilize and undergo transformation.

The research focuses on problems concerning **psychic and collective individuation, affective experience, the constitution of identity, desire, anxiety and subjective transformation**.

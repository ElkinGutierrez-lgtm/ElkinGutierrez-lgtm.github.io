I am a philosopher and researcher. My work lies at the intersection of continental philosophy, ontology, philosophy of technology, phenomenology and legal theory.

My research begins with a transversal question: **how individuals, objects, institutions and worlds become constituted through relations that precede and transform them.**

## Education and research interests

My philosophical work focuses primarily on individuation, ontogenesis, technology, existence, identity and institution.

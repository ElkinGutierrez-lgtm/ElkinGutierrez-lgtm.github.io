# LABPROB

## Laboratory of Problematics

**Problematic fields, individuation, and transformation**

The Laboratory of Problematics investigates the genesis, structure, persistence, and transformation of problematic fields that condition the emergence of new forms of existence, thought, organization, and action.

Its point of departure is not the already constituted entity, but the field of tensions that makes its emergence possible.

> **Study problems. Understand emergences. Invent transformations.**

---

## The Problem Before the Object

Research ordinarily begins with already constituted objects: individuals, institutions, technologies, concepts, organisms, or systems.

LABPROB proposes reversing this direction.

Rather than asking what an entity is, we ask:

**What problematic field makes its individuation possible?**

A problematic field comprises tensions, incompatibilities, potentials, constraints, relations, and processes of transformation.

From this perspective, individuals are provisional results of processes of individuation.

Problems, by contrast, constitute genetic conditions.

---

## Fundamental Question

**How do problematic fields transform, and what new forms of existence can emerge from their internal tensions?**
